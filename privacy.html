Privacy Policy

Pictensify

Last updated: October 2026

Pictensify (“we”, “the app”) is a photo editor that processes images on your device.

1. Photos & edits
Photos you import, capture, or edit stay on your device unless you choose to share or save them using the system share sheet or Photos. We do not operate a server that receives your photos for editing.

2. Advertising
If you use the free version, we show ads from Google AdMob (Google LLC). AdMob and its partners may collect device and advertising identifiers, IP address, coarse location, interaction data, and diagnostic information to deliver, measure, and improve ads. This may include personalized ads where allowed.

You can limit ad tracking via iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and (where required) via in-app privacy options for ads.

Google’s privacy information: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. App Tracking Transparency
On supported iOS versions we may ask for permission to track activity across other companies’ apps and websites for personalized advertising. You can refuse; the app still works, and ads may be less relevant. ATT is for advertising, not required to edit photos.

4. Subscriptions & purchases
Purchases and restores are handled by Apple. We receive entitlement status from StoreKit (for example, whether Pictensify Pro is active) so we can remove ads. We do not receive your full payment card details.

5. Analytics (Firebase)
We use Google Firebase Analytics (Google LLC) to understand how the app is used — for example which tools are opened, when export/share happens, and subscription funnel events. This helps us improve Pictensify.

Analytics may process identifiers such as an app-instance ID, device/app info, approximate region derived from IP, and interaction events. Events do not include your photo contents or the text of photos.

Firebase / Google Analytics information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

We do not show a separate Firebase consent popup. Where Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) form is required (for example EEA/UK), that form covers the regional consent path for ads; analytics collection follows that path. Elsewhere, analytics runs as disclosed here after app start. ATT is for personalized ads, not required for product analytics. Core editing works offline on your device.

6. Children
The app is not directed to children under 13. Do not use the app if you are under the age required by local law.
